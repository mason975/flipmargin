Etsy seller fees explained: how to calculate your real profit

Understand the standard US Etsy Payments fees, spot additional charges, and estimate what remains after shipping, processing, listings, and advertising.

Open the free reseller profit calculator →

By FlipMargin Editorial · Updated November 5, 2026 · Practical guide for US resellers

The three standard Etsy fee components

For a US seller using Etsy Payments, the main fees to model are the transaction fee, payment processing, and listing fee. Etsy's official documentation currently lists a 6.5% transaction fee and US payment processing of 3% plus $0.25 per order. Together, the percentage components equal 9.5%, with the fixed $0.25 processing fee separate. A standard listing costs $0.20.

  • Transaction: 6.5% of the total order amount, including shipping and gift wrap charged to the buyer.
  • Payment processing: 3% + $0.25 for US bank accounts; the processing base includes shipping and applicable sales tax.
  • Listing: $0.20 when a listing is created. A listing generally expires after four months and may incur renewal charges.

See Etsy's official fees and taxes guide and payment processing fee table.

How FlipMargin estimates Etsy fees

The Etsy preset uses 9.5%, $0.25, and $0.20. It applies the combined percentage to item price plus buyer-paid shipping, and adds the fixed processing and listing fees. It does not include sales tax in the modeled percentage base. Since Etsy says payment processing applies to applicable sales tax, FlipMargin can understate the processing portion when tax applies.

Important: 9.5% is a convenient combined estimate, not one Etsy fee. The 6.5% transaction and 3% processing portions have different policy bases. In particular, the processing fee can include applicable sales tax.

Example: estimate the profit on an Etsy sale

Suppose an item costs $20 to acquire, sells for $60, and the buyer pays $5 shipping. You spend $6 on the shipping label and $1 on packaging. Using the standard US Etsy preset:

  • Modeled order revenue: $60 + $5 = $65.00
  • Combined percentage estimate: $65 × 9.5% = $6.18
  • Payment processing fixed fee: $0.25
  • Listing fee: $0.20
  • Purchase, shipping, and packaging: $20 + $6 + $1 = $27.00
  • Estimated profit: $65 − $6.18 − $0.25 − $0.20 − $27 = $31.37

This is a simplified estimate. It excludes applicable sales tax from the processing base and assumes one listing quantity, no Offsite Ads attribution, no currency conversion, and no other shop-specific charges.

Additional Etsy fees that can change the result

Offsite Ads

For an order attributed to an Offsite Ad, Etsy currently lists a 15% fee for shops below $10,000 in Etsy sales during the previous 365 days and 12% for shops at or above that threshold. The fee is capped at $100 per order. Whether participation is optional depends on the shop's sales history and eligibility rules. Model the actual attributed fee separately.

Multiple quantities and listing renewals

Etsy charges $0.20 per listing. If multiple quantities from one listing sell in the same order, an additional $0.20 applies for each extra quantity. Renewals can also create additional listing fees.

Other possible costs

  • Etsy Ads campaign spend.
  • Currency conversion, currently 2.5% when Etsy converts listing currency to the payment-account currency.
  • One-time shop setup fee, if Etsy presents one during onboarding.
  • Shipping labels purchased through Etsy, where available.
  • Regulatory operating fees in certain countries; Etsy's current policy lists countries where these apply.
  • Taxes on seller fees where applicable.

How to price an item for Etsy

  1. Estimate a realistic item price using comparable sold listings and your product's condition.
  2. Decide whether shipping is included in the price or charged separately; either way, include the actual postage cost in your calculation.
  3. Use the US Etsy Payments preset if your payment bank account is in the United States.
  4. Add a promotion fee only when it applies to the order. For Offsite Ads, use the rate that matches your shop's threshold and remember the per-order cap.
  5. Account for listing renewals and additional quantities where relevant.
  6. Check the official Etsy fee pages before changing prices or buying inventory.

Frequently asked questions

Is Etsy's US fee 9.5%?

The standard transaction and payment-processing percentages add to 9.5% for US Etsy Payments: 6.5% + 3%. The $0.25 processing fee and $0.20 listing fee are separate.

Does Etsy charge its percentage fees on shipping?

The transaction fee applies to shipping charged to the buyer, and payment processing also includes shipping in its base.

Does Etsy payment processing include sales tax?

Etsy's help documentation says applicable sales tax is included in the payment-processing base. FlipMargin's current preset excludes it, so the estimate may be low.
