How eBay selling fees are generally calculated

For most US sellers, eBay charges a final value fee made up of a percentage of the total sale amount plus a per-order fee. The total sale amount can include the item price, shipping charged to the buyer, sales tax, and certain other amounts. The exact fee percentage depends on the product category and seller account.

FlipMargin's standard eBay preset is a simplified estimate for most categories and sellers without a Store. It uses 13.6% plus a $0.30 per-order fee for orders of $10 or less, or $0.40 for orders above $10. It models percentage fees on item price plus buyer-paid shipping, and does not calculate sales tax.

What can change your eBay fee?

  • Category: some categories have different final value fee rates or thresholds.
  • Store subscription: Store subscribers may have different fee schedules.
  • Order total: the fixed per-order fee depends on the applicable total-order threshold.
  • Promoted listings: advertising fees may apply to attributed sales.
  • Listing volume: insertion fees may apply after any free monthly listing allowance is used.

Frequently asked questions

Does eBay charge the fee on shipping?

For many fee calculations, the total sale amount includes shipping charged to the buyer. The applicable base and exceptions should be checked in eBay's fee table for your category and account.

Is 13.6% the rate for every eBay category?

No. It is a simplified standard preset for most categories. Some categories and Store plans use different rates or fee rules.

Does FlipMargin include sales tax in the eBay fee estimate?

No. The current calculator models percentage fees on item price plus buyer-paid shipping and excludes sales tax. It can therefore understate fees when tax is part of the applicable fee base.
