The standard US Etsy fee components

For US sellers using Etsy Payments, the standard transaction fee is 6.5% of the order amount, and US payment processing is 3% plus $0.25 per transaction. That creates a simplified combined percentage of 9.5%, plus the $0.25 processing charge. Etsy also charges a $0.20 listing fee per listing, including when a listing renews or additional quantities trigger renewal.

FlipMargin's Etsy preset models 9.5%, $0.25, and $0.20. Its percentage base is item price plus buyer-paid shipping; it does not calculate sales tax, so the payment-processing estimate may be low when tax applies.

Other Etsy fees to consider

  • Offsite Ads: attributed orders can incur a 15% fee for shops below the applicable $10,000 sales threshold, or 12% for shops at or above it; a per-order cap applies.
  • Etsy Ads: advertising spend varies with the campaign and clicks.
  • Currency conversion: a conversion fee may apply when listing and payment-account currencies differ.
  • Setup and regulatory fees: additional charges may apply depending on the seller's circumstances and location.

Frequently asked questions

Is Etsy's US fee 9.5%?

For a US Etsy Payments seller, 6.5% transaction plus 3% payment processing gives a 9.5% combined percentage for a simplified estimate. The $0.25 processing fee and $0.20 listing fee are separate.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Etsy's transaction and payment-processing calculations can include shipping charged to the buyer. The calculator includes buyer-paid shipping in its modeled percentage base.

Does FlipMargin include sales tax?

No. The current Etsy preset excludes sales tax from its modeled percentage base. Since payment processing can apply to tax amounts, the estimate may understate that portion.

Are Offsite Ads included automatically?

No. Offsite Ads are optional in the model; enter an appropriate promotion percentage when applicable. The actual rate depends on the shop's sales threshold and order attribution.
